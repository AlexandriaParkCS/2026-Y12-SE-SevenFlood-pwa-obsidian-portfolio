# Project Journal

**Date:** 24 Feb 2026  
**Project:** Loyal Cafe Rewards PWA  
**Module:** Interface design and storyboard

## What I Did

- Planned the first user journey for the loyalty app.
- Created a storyboard showing the home page, sign-up page and dashboard.
- Decided that the first screen should be simple, with clear Login and Sign Up options.

## Why I Did It

The storyboard helped me plan the app before writing code. It showed what the user would see first and how they would move from creating an account to checking their loyalty rewards.

## Challenges

The main challenge was keeping the design simple while still showing the important features. I did not want the first screen to feel crowded or confusing.

## How I Solved Them

I focused the storyboard on the most important customer actions: joining the loyalty program, logging in and viewing progress. Extra features like rewards and history could be added after the main flow worked.

## Next Steps

- Plan the database structure.
- Decide what data needs to be stored for users, points, rewards and transactions.
- Start connecting the design to the Flask application.

## Reflection

This stage helped me understand that a good app needs a clear user flow before implementation. The storyboard became a guide for the pages I needed to build later.
