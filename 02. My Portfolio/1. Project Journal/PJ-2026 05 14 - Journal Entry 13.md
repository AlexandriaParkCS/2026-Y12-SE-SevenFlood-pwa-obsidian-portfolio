# Project Journal

**Date:** 14 May 2026  
**Project:** Loyal Cafe Rewards PWA  
**Module:** Visual design and mobile refinement

## What I Did

- Improved the cafe-themed visual style.
- Refined the dashboard, signup, login, rewards and history pages.
- Improved mobile spacing so cards, forms and buttons fit better on small screens.
- Added clearer logo and app styling.

## Why I Did It

The app needs to work well on phones because customers will usually access a loyalty card from a mobile device. A clear layout also makes the app feel more professional.

## Challenges

The challenge was making the interface look polished without making it too busy. Some spacing and form layout issues were more noticeable on mobile.

## How I Solved Them

I used responsive styling, card layouts and clearer spacing. I also improved the logo presentation so it was easier to see against the cafe colour theme.

## Next Steps

- Capture final desktop and mobile screenshots.
- Add those screenshots into the portfolio.
- Use feedback from testers to make final improvements.

## Reflection

This stage reminded me that functionality and design both matter. Even if the code works, users still need pages that are readable, clear and easy to use.
